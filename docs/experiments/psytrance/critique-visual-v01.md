# Visual pilot v01 — independent critique

Decision: REVISE BEFORE FULL RENDER. Underlying monochrome dotted geometry direction can continue; this is not final audiovisual approval.

Evidence personally inspected: `runs/psytrance-visual-stills-v01/contact-sheet.png` and fullsize `still-100.png`, `still-310.png`, `still-370.png`, `still-480.png`. The reel in `runs/psytrance-visual-preview-v01/preview-silent.mp4` exists; its metadata says 960 frames at 30 fps and `actual_audio: false`. I have not watched it as continuous motion. The stills use fallback timing, not actual music.

## Required revisions

1. **Compositional sameness across the timeline.** Most of the twelve sampled phases present a detached central object with broadly similar framing. The Mandelbulb motif returns repeatedly with little visible compositional transformation. Establish selected immersive, cropped, asymmetrical views at peaks, with clearly contrasting sparse sections. Do not merely enlarge everything: the quiet sections need room to breathe, and the climax needs a reason to feel larger.
2. **Main-body hierarchy at 100s and 310s.** Small satellite rings/snowflakes are brighter than the main body. They pull the eye toward decorative objects at the edges and weaken the sense of a single recursive system. Integrate them through a legible nesting/depth relationship, make them subordinate, or remove them in close views.
3. **Gray-fuzz appearance at 480s.** Individual dots are visible in the fullsize Mandelbulb, but the fit-to-screen view loses its multiscale structure. Strengthen selected structural/contour dot tiers and a small number of luminous highlights. Global exposure alone would flatten the depth into a bright cloud. Preserve separation between bright foreground, middle structure and dark recesses.

## Direction that can be retained

The inspected frames are genuinely monochrome and assembled from visible dots. The pentagonal recursive form at 310s is more immediately legible as fractal than the torus at 100s, whose carpet structure becomes secondary to the donut silhouette. The curved, directional form at 370s provides useful compositional contrast. These observations establish a viable palette and geometry vocabulary, not sufficient ten-minute development or motion quality.

## Next evidence required

- Revised contact sheet, especially the first drop, Event Horizon and final peak, plus sparse Suspension for contrast.
- Closely spaced frame sequences through representative movement and family transitions, not only one still per section.
- Actual audio-derived timing before synchronization judgment.
- Full-duration output before final scoring. No audio, groove, mix, smoothness or shimmer score is awarded in this still-only review.

Sent the required revisions directly to the visual worker and coordinator. The worker had already independently begun scale/crop changes; review findings remain applicable to the original v01 evidence.
