# Gujora / Yundol — spatial camera version

This is a new rendering mode, separate from the earlier moving-particle version. It converts bright pixels from the accepted no-people cover into 3D points and short neighboring filaments. The cover supplies geometry and is not displayed as a flat textured plane. A reverse perspective construction places the original lines at their original screen coordinates from the opening camera.

The water points lie on an actual horizontal plane. Island contours occupy multiple depths, with an independent rounded terrain volume that becomes visible at oblique angles and a sparse interior layer along the source rays. People, a cylindrical brazier, fire, smoke and selected organic connecting curves are additional spatial objects. There are seven independently constructed people in three groups: three men, a couple, and a mother and child. Driftwood is on the landward portion of the beach. The island and the new elements are artistic constructions, not a surveyed model or a trained Gaussian Splat reconstruction.

Every particle's world position is fixed. No particle curl, explosion, procedural breathing or time-driven geometric displacement is applied. A moving perspective camera provides the changes in alignment and parallax; smooth audio-band envelopes affect light intensity only. Small elliptical soft point sprites with perspective size attenuation, depth-tested occlusion and distance dimming provide a splat-like appearance. A few fixed foreground strands offer close-pass detail. On return to the opening camera, the source-derived lines optically realign without particles flying back.

The normalized camera route holds the opening view through 15%, approaches the shoreline at 35%, enters the wave field at 45%, reaches a substantial side view at 55%, passes within the smoke volume at 67%, rises toward the sky at 75%, and returns to the opening view by 95%. The island is intentionally allowed to leave the frame in the close passages. One authoritative keyframe list drives the camera and is recorded in metadata. The renderer marks `geometry.staticParticlePositions` in metadata. These are implementation intentions; actual motion and visual quality require preview review.

The default is 464 seconds at 24 fps: 11,136 frames, 1080 × 1080, H.264/AAC. Use `node tools/gujora-spatial.mjs --input=PATH --out=PATH` for the full film. `--duration-total=SECONDS` changes the total timeline if supplied audio and feature frames cover it. `--preview` produces a 12-second excerpt near 40%; `--start` and `--duration` choose another frame-aligned interval. `--stills-only --still-times=60,160,250,350,440` renders five evaluation moments without encoding the film. `--cover=PATH` changes the source image.

The output refuses to overwrite video, stills and its snapshots. A completed export records the source PNG, WAV, audio features, scene and renderer SHA-256 hashes; `reference.png`, `scene.mjs` and `renderer.mjs` preserve their exact input bytes. Preview and still-only artifacts use separately named reference files. A real rendered preview must be checked before describing the 3D effect or render performance as verified.
