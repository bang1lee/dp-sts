## V01

[첫 시안](stills/holistic-sea-v01.png). 넓은 수면층이 쌓인 인상이 강해, 공간 속 군집의 표현을 수정할 대상으로 기록했다.

실제 생성 프롬프트:

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: text-free art-direction still, landscape 16:9.
Primary request: a holistic experience of looking at sun glitter on gentle ocean waves, but the waterlike glint field lifts into a three-dimensional volume surrounding the observer. Ocean-ness must remain unmistakable. This is NOT generic floating particles, a cosmos, or a beach postcard.
Scene: sea light, a quiet rippled ocean continuous into the distance, a restrained trace of wet sand low at the edge. The main subject is thin gently undulating waterlike ripple patches distributed at several heights and depths, some near eye level, floating as extensions of the same ocean wave rhythm. Surface orientations and perspective foreshortening strongly suggest water. No solid magical waves or floating glass shards.
Glint clusters: several loose roughly circular or elliptical envelopes of naturally fragmented sun reflections, composed of many small irregular flecks following curved wavelet ridges. Uneven occupancy, breathing gaps, dense little islands separated by quiet water. These envelopes are made only by the distribution of tiny reflected highlights; NEVER outline circles or draw luminous rings, portals, spheres, bubbles or round lenses. Their softly rounded groupings feel like the patches of sun glitter one sees looking out over the sea. Only a small portion of the total ripple field is brightly reflecting at this captured viewpoint. Fewer brighter flecks, more subdued reflective facets.
Composition: observer camera INSIDE a spacious INVISIBLE cubic volume near one corner looking diagonally across its interior, with a nearby ripple grouping off-axis, mid-distance groupings and distant clusters. No visible cube, edges, cage, walls or room. The wave ridges and fragment orientation are shared coherently across depth; groups appear connected through interrupted flowing wave structures, not network lines. A camera shift would reveal other reflecting facets; do not merely billboard circular graphics toward the camera. Large quiet gaps and restrained locally nested ripple detail; avoid excessive recursive detail.
Natural sea and sand tones and coastal daylight are a PROVISIONAL visual interpretation. Soft but directional light, local controlled specular reflections, legible material detail, a quiet immersive cinematic atmosphere. No overexposed center, glowing tunnel, nebula, glitter fog, jellyfish, starburst stars, jewelry, people or objects unrelated to water and sand.
No text, no gauges, no frame, no diagrams, no symbols. This is a static proposal for spatial and material feel, not proof of animation or real-time interaction.
